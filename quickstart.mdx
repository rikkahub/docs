title: "Quickstart: Send Your First AI Message in 5 Minutes"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"

---

This guide gets you from zero to your first AI conversation as quickly as possible. You need an Android device, a copy of Rikka installed, and an API key from at least one AI provider. If you do not have an API key yet, sign up with your chosen provider first — links are included in the steps below.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download and Install Rikka">
    Install Rikka on your Android device from either the Google Play Store or the official website.

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card
        title="Google Play Store"
        icon="rocket"
        href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=me.rerere.rikkahub"
      >
        Tap **Install** on the Play listing for automatic updates and easy installation.
      </Card>
      <Card
        title="Official Website"
        icon="download"
        href="https://rikka-ai.com/download"
      >
        Download the APK directly from rikka-ai.com if the Play Store is unavailable in your region.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>

    Once installation is complete, open Rikka from your home screen or app drawer.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Settings and Add a Provider">
    Rikka needs at least one AI provider before you can chat. Navigate to the provider settings now.

    1. Tap the **Settings** icon (gear) in the bottom navigation bar.
    2. Select **Providers** from the settings menu.
    3. Tap **Add Provider** and choose your provider type from the list.

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="OpenAI">
        Select **OpenAI** from the provider list. OpenAI powers models like GPT-4o and GPT-4.1.

        Get your API key at [platform.openai.com/api-keys](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys).
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Google Gemini">
        Select **Google Gemini** from the provider list. Gemini powers models like Gemini 2.5 Pro and Gemini 2.0 Flash.

        Get your API key at [aistudio.google.com/app/apikey](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey).
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Anthropic Claude">
        Select **Anthropic Claude** from the provider list. Claude powers models like Claude Sonnet and Claude Opus.

        Get your API key at [console.anthropic.com/settings/keys](https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys).
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="OpenAI-Compatible">
        Select **OpenAI-Compatible** from the provider list to connect any endpoint that follows the OpenAI API format — such as Ollama, OpenRouter, Mistral, or a self-hosted model.

        Enter the **Base URL** of your endpoint (for example, `http://localhost:11434/v1` for a local Ollama instance) along with your API key if required.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Your API Key">
    Paste your API key into the **API Key** field for the provider you selected.

    <Warning>
      Treat your API key like a password. Do not share it, and do not paste it into untrusted apps. Rikka stores your key locally on your device and never sends it to Rikka's servers — it goes directly to your chosen provider.
    </Warning>

    After pasting your key, tap **Save** or **Confirm** to store the provider configuration. Rikka validates the key in the background and displays a green checkmark when the connection is successful.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a Model">
    With your provider connected, choose the model you want to use for your first conversation.

    1. From the **Providers** screen, tap the provider you just added.
    2. Under **Default Model**, tap the model selector and browse the available models.
    3. Choose a model — a good starting point is listed below for each provider.

    | Provider | Recommended Starter Model |
    |---|---|
    | OpenAI | `gpt-4o-mini` |
    | Google Gemini | `gemini-2.0-flash` |
    | Anthropic Claude | `claude-3-5-haiku` |
    | OpenAI-Compatible | Depends on your endpoint |

    <Note>
      You can change the model at any time from within a conversation using the model picker in the chat toolbar. Your default model is just the starting point for new chats.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start Your First Conversation">
    You are ready to chat. Head back to the main screen and open a new conversation.

    1. Tap the **Chat** icon in the bottom navigation bar to return to the conversation list.
    2. Tap the **New Chat** button (pencil or plus icon) in the top-right corner.
    3. Confirm that the correct provider and model are shown in the chat toolbar. Tap them to switch if needed.
    4. Type your first message in the text field at the bottom and tap **Send**.

    Rikka streams the response back to you in real time. That's it — you're up and running!

    <Tip>
      Try a simple opener like *"Explain what you can help me with"* to get a feel for the model's capabilities before diving into more specific tasks.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Rikka supports OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, and any OpenAI-compatible API. See the **[Providers Overview](/providers/overview)** to learn how to add multiple providers and switch between them mid-conversation.
</Tip>
